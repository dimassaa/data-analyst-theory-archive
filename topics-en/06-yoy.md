# 📆 Year-over-year (YoY)

🌐 [Русская версия](../topics/06-yoy.md)

**YoY (year-over-year)** — comparing a metric with the same period of the previous year. Shows how the metric has changed over a year.

**Formula:**
$$
\text{YoY (\%)} = \frac{\text{value of current period} - \text{value of the same period last year}}{\text{value of the same period last year}} \times 100
$$

**Example:**
Revenue in October 2025 is 500 thousand RUB, in October 2024 — 400 thousand RUB.
YoY = (500-400)/400 × 100 = **+25%**.

**Why use YoY:**
- Removes **seasonality**: the same periods of the year are compared (October with October).
- Gives an "honest" picture of long-term business growth that doesn't depend on the time of year.

**Limitations:**
- Doesn't see the most recent changes within the year — if the business grew over a month, YoY will only show it over time.
- Sensitive to last year's anomalies (if there was a collapse last year, YoY will be greatly inflated).

**Key takeaway:** for a business with strong seasonality (retail, tourism, e-commerce), YoY is the main comparison tool because it removes the influence of holidays and seasonal peaks. MoM and WoW are also used for operational monitoring.
