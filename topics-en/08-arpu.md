# 👥 ARPU (Average Revenue Per User)
🌐 [Русская версия](../topics/08-arpu.md)

**ARPU (Average Revenue Per User)** — the average revenue from one user over a certain period.

**Formula:**
$$
\text{ARPU} = \frac{\text{revenue for the period}}{\text{number of users for the period}}
$$

**Variants:**
- **ARPPU** — revenue from a paying user (paying users in the denominator).
- **ARPDAU** — revenue from an active user per day.

**Example:**
Monthly revenue — 1,000,000 RUB, users — 50,000.
ARPU = 1,000,000 / 50,000 = **20 RUB per user per month**.

**What to look at:**
- ARPU is useful for comparing monetization across segments, regions, and platforms.
- ARPU growth can come from higher prices, upsells, or a smaller share of free users.
- Do not confuse it with ARPPU: if almost no one pays, ARPU is low, although ARPPU may be high.

**Relationship with other metrics:**
- **LTV** ≈ ARPU × customer lifetime.
- ARPU is a point-in-time metric for a month; LTV is cumulative over the relationship.

**Insight:** ARPU is useful when there is a stable user base and is more often analyzed by segment (paying/free, regions, cohorts), since averages "over everyone" hide the pace of segmentation. Do not forget to check how many users actually pay, so that you interpret the dynamics correctly.
