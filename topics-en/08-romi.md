# 📣 ROMI (Return on Marketing Investment)
🌐 [Русская версия](../topics/08-romi.md)

**ROMI (Return on Marketing Investment)** — ROI calculated specifically for marketing investments: how much profit each ruble spent on marketing brings.

**Formula:**
$$
\text{ROMI} (\%) = \frac{\text{marketing revenue} - \text{marketing costs}}{\text{marketing costs}} \times 100
$$

**Example:**
Spent 200,000 RUB on a campaign. Revenue directly attributed to the campaign — 350,000 RUB.
ROMI = (350,000 - 200,000) / 200,000 × 100 = **75%**.

**How ROMI differs from ROI:**
- ROMI accounts only for **marketing** costs and the revenue obtained thanks to them.
- ROI is a more general indicator covering any investments (product, equipment, IT).

**Attribution problems:**
- It is not always clear which revenue exactly "belongs" to the campaign (attribution models, offline), so the ROMI figure can be an estimate.
- When comparing with ROI for marketing, ROMI is often used specifically so that costs are not mixed together.

**Insight:** ROMI allows comparing the effectiveness of different marketing channels and making decisions about budget reallocation. Always clarify the calculation period and the attribution model, otherwise the figures are incomparable.
