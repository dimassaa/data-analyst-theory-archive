# 🏗️ Funnels and Cohorts

🌐 [Русская версия](../topics/09-funnels-and-cohorts.md)

**Funnel** — the user's path to a target action (purchase, sign-up), split into stages. **Cohort** — a group of users united by a common characteristic (usually the date of first action). Together these tools form the foundation of product analytics: a funnel shows where users are lost, while cohorts show how the behavior of groups changes over time.

## 📊 How the tools complement each other

| Role | Funnel | Cohorts |
|------|---------|---------|
| Question | "Where are users lost?" | "Has it gotten better over time?" |
| Slice | By stages of the user journey | By time of first activity (week/month) |
| Detection tool | Bottlenecks, drop-offs between stages | Retention trends, release effects |
| Typical conclusion | "Step 3 loses 60%" | "The March cohort retains better than January" |

---

## ✅ Summary

- The funnel answers the question "where and how much do we lose", while cohorts answer "are users behaving better over time".
- Cohort analysis is often used to assess retention (see [Retention](08-retention.md)) and the impact of releases.
- Funnels without accounting for time can be misleading (a user is not obliged to visit the site every day).
