# 📉 Reading charts and graphs

🌐 [Русская версия](../topics/06-reading-charts.md)

The ability to read charts is a basic analyst skill: it is on charts that anomalies, trends and outliers are spotted.

**Main types of charts and when they are used:**
- **Line chart** — the dynamics of a metric over time.
- **Bar chart** — comparing categories.
- **Histogram** — the distribution of a continuous quantity (frequencies by interval).
- **Box plot** — distribution: median, quartiles, outliers.
- **Scatter plot** — the relationship between two variables.

**What to look for on a chart:**
1. **Trend** — the general direction of movement (up, down, flat).
2. **Seasonality** — repeating cycles (weekly, monthly).
3. **Outliers** — anomalous points that stand out sharply from the overall series.
4. **Spread** — the volatility of the metric.

**Mistakes when reading charts:**
- Non-comparable axes (axes that don't start at zero can visually exaggerate differences).
- Different axis scales when comparing two charts.
- A time interval that's too compressed — instead of a trend you see "noise".

**Key takeaway:** for any data analysis, start with visualization. Outliers and errors in the data are often visible to the eye before you start calculating.
