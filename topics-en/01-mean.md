# 📊 Mean (arithmetic)

🌐 [Русская версия](../topics/01-mean.md)

**Mean** — the sum of all values divided by their count. It is the most common measure of central tendency.

**Formula:**
$$
\text{mean} = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{n} x_i}{n}
$$

**Example:**
Salaries of five employees: 30, 35, 40, 45, 100 thousand RUB.
Mean = (30+35+40+45+100)/5 = 250/5 = **50 thousand RUB**

**Features:**
- Sensitive to outliers (abnormally large or small values). In the example, the mean is "inflated" by the single salary of 100 thousand.
- Works well for symmetric distributions without strong deviations.

**Key takeaway:** when outliers are present, the mean can be misleading — in such cases compare it with the median.
