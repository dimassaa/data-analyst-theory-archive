# 💸 CAC (Cost of Acquisition)
🌐 [Русская версия](../topics/08-cac.md)

**CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost)** — the average cost of acquiring one new customer.

**Formula:**
$$
\text{CAC} = \frac{\text{acquisition costs (marketing + sales)}}{\text{number of acquired customers}}
$$

**Example:**
An advertising campaign cost 300,000 RUB and acquired 150 new customers.
CAC = 300,000 / 150 = **2000 RUB per customer**.

**Important nuances:**
- Include **all** costs related to acquisition: advertising, salaries of marketers and the sales department, tools, landing page maintenance.
- Payback period: how many months it takes for a customer to "recoup" their CAC.
- It should be calculated **separately by acquisition channel** to understand which channel is more effective.

**Relationship with LTV:**
- The requirement for sustainable unit economics: $ \text{LTV} > \text{CAC} $.
- A benchmark for short-term businesses: LTV / CAC ≥ 3 is considered a good indicator.

**Examples of mistakes:**
- Failing to account for salaries and overhead costs.
- Including all site visitors in the denominator rather than only customers who actually paid.

**Insight:** CAC never exists "in a vacuum" — it must be compared with LTV and tracked over time by channel. Rising CAC with the same LTV is a signal of deteriorating channel efficiency or market saturation.
