# 📈 ROI (Return on Investment)
🌐 [Русская версия](../topics/08-roi.md)

**ROI (Return on Investment)** — the investment payback ratio: how much net profit (or loss) the invested funds bring.

**Formula:**
$$
\text{ROI} (\%) = \frac{\text{revenue} - \text{costs}}{\text{costs}} \times 100
$$

**Example:**
Invested 100,000 RUB in a project, received 150,000 RUB in revenue.
ROI = (150,000 - 100,000) / 100,000 × 100 = **50%**.

or

Invested 100,000, received 80,000.
ROI = (80,000 - 100,000) / 100,000 × 100 = **-20%** (loss).

**Interpretation:**
- ROI > 0 — the investment paid off, there is profit.
- ROI < 0 — the investment is unprofitable.
- ROI = 0 — the funds returned, but without profit.

**Things to clarify:**
- Which period we consider (quarterly ROI ≠ annual ROI).
- What counts as "revenue" and "costs" — unify the definition.

**Usage example:**
Compare two acquisition channels: channel A's ROI = 40%, channel B's = 15% → channel A is more profitable, all else being equal.

**Insight:** ROI is convenient for comparing alternative investments, but it does not account for the time of capital return or project size (a large project with a 10% ROI can yield more profit than a small one with 100% ROI). For marketing, a refined version is used — ROMI.
