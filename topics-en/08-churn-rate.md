# 🚪 Churn rate
🌐 [Русская версия](../topics/08-churn-rate.md)

**Churn rate** — the share of customers who stopped using the product over a certain period (month, quarter, year).

**Formula:**
$$
\text{Churn} (\%) = \frac{\text{customers who stopped using}}{\text{all customers at the start of the period}} \times 100
$$

**Example:**
At the start of the month there are 10,000 subscribers. Over the month, 300 canceled their subscription.
Churn = 300/10,000 × 100 = **3% per month**.

**Retention and churn:**
For a stable period in a subscription business:
$$
\text{Churn} \approx 1 - \text{Retention} \quad \text{(for the same period)}
$$
(approximately, with a stable base).

**How churn affects the business:**
- Directly affects LTV: churn = 3% → average customer lifetime = 1/0.03 ≈ 33 months.
- Determines how much must be spent on acquisition so that the base does not shrink.
- Even a small reduction in churn noticeably increases LTV and revenue.

**How churn is reduced:**
- Improving onboarding and product quality.
- Support and loyalty programs.
- Identifying "at-risk" users by activity (e.g., a drop in usage frequency).

**Insight:** churn = 5% per month seems low, but 90% of customers leave within ~45 months, and a business should assess not the "uniqueness" of the number, but its impact on LTV and the necessary acquisition spend. Reducing churn is one of the most profitable levers for profit growth.
